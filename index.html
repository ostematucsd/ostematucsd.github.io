Our Mission

At oSTEM, we are dedicated to fostering a supportive and inclusive community for LGBTQ+ individuals in STEM. Through mentorship, professional development, and social events, we empower students to thrive academically, professionally, and personally.

Diversity Statement

In accordance with applicable federal and state law and University policy, oSTEM @ UC San Diego does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, disability, and/or other protected categories. All enrolled students are encouraged to participate in our events and activities.
